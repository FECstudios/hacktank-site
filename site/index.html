How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in with email

    We send you a one-time login link. No passwords to remember or leak.

  2. 2

    Connect your TikTok

    Through TikTok's official Login Kit. It proves the account is yours, so nobody can add someone else's profile.

  3. 3

    Watch and guess

    Scroll through ten videos a hidden player reposted, like a tiny For You page. Then slide to guess their IQ.

  4. 4

    See the reveal

    Find out who it was, their real in-game IQ and how your guess moved their score. Next person.

Fair, crowd-made scores

Everyone starts at 100. Your in-game IQ is shaped by what other players guess after watching your reposts. It's a game score, not a real IQ test.

  • Smarter guessers count more. A player's guess is weighted by their own in-game IQ, from 0.1× up to 2×.
  • Trolls don't count. Guesses that are wildly off are ignored automatically.
  • Scores settle over time. The more guesses someone gets, the less any single guess can move them.
  • One guess per person. You can't guess the same player twice, or yourself.

Privacy & safety

Only what's needed

From TikTok we only receive your username, display name and profile picture. We never see your TikTok password.

No video downloads

Videos play through TikTok's official embed player and stay on TikTok. We only store which videos to show.

Your data, your call

Delete your account any time from the game settings. Deleting removes your TikTok link, reposts, guesses and scores.

No bullying

Harassing players over their scores breaks our rules and gets accounts removed. Report abuse to craftfec@gmail.com.

FAQ

Is this a real IQ test?

No. The "IQ" in tiq is a playful game score that comes from other players' guesses. It says nothing scientific about anyone.

What does tiq see from my TikTok account?

Your username, display name and profile picture (via TikTok Login Kit), plus the IDs of your 10 most recent public reposts so other players can watch them. Nothing private, no messages, no password.

Who can see my reposts?

Other tiq players see your public reposts while guessing, without your name. Your identity is only shown after they submit a guess.

How do I delete my account?

In the game, open the menu → settings → "delete my account". You can also email us at craftfec@gmail.com.

Is tiq made by TikTok?

No. tiq is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok.
