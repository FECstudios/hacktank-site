Guess someone's IQ from their TikTok reposts.

  1. Sign in with email and connect your TikTok account.
  2. Take a short puzzle test to get your starting in-game IQ.
  3. Watch another player's reposts and guess their IQ.
  4. See their score. The smarter you are, the more your guess counts.
